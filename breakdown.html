What this solves

Most small service businesses don’t need more random software. They need one messy workflow cleaned up.

For local service businesses, that workflow is often lead response: who called, who still needs a callback, who asked for an estimate, what’s been answered, and what’s still sitting there cooling off.

Missed callbacks

Leads come in while work is happening, then follow-up gets delayed or forgotten.

Scattered follow-up

Texts, calls, Facebook messages, and quote requests live in too many places.

No clean next-step visibility

Too much of the process depends on memory instead of a repeatable system.

The starter setup focuses on one thing: fixing the lead-response workflow.
